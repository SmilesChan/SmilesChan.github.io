Served as session chairman at SAES2024

12th International Symposium on Applied Engineering and Sciences

Thrilled to share that I had the honor of chairing a session on Engineering & Technology at the 12th International Symposium on Applied Engineering and Sciences (SAES2024), held at the Faculty of Engineering, Kyushu Institute of Technology!

SAES2024 is an international forum for presentations and interactions in the current and emerging areas of Applied Engineering and Sciences. It also provides a unique platform for researchers, students, scientist and engineers to share their experiences and views of their latest research breakthrough.

Grateful for the opportunity to be part of such an inspiring event and to witness the exciting research shaping the future of technology. Looking forward to seeing how these discussions will continue to drive innovation and collaboration in the years to come!

Wentao Chen
Associate Professor

My research interests include extreme near-field heat transfer, thermal transport at the solid-solid and solid-liquid interface, and molecular dynamics simulation.
