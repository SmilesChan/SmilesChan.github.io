Japan-Asia Youth Exchange Program in Science

Online Sakura Science 2024

From October 2024 to January 2025, Kyutech organized an online international exchange program titled “Global Engineering Education in Mechanical Engineering Field through Online Project Based Learning.” This initiative was part of the Sakura Science Plan, supported by Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST).

The program attracted 47 participants, comprising 36 students and 11 faculty members from institutions including Qingdao University of Technology, Northeastern University (CN), Shandong University, University of Malaya, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology, Yanshan University, Qilu University of Technology, Indian Institute of Technology, Guwahati, and University of Southern California.

International Joint Seminar on Mechanical Engineering 2024 was held to promote academic exchange in mechanical engineering field. The participants enjoyed 6 keynote lectures and 42 oral presentations through Zoom. Online events such as campus/laboratory tours, and collaborative works have been conducted by 36 foreign students together with 21 Japanese students. All participants worked diligently with their teams to complete the projects “Spin” and “Magnetic” successfully. This online event fostered international cooperation and strengthened friendships among all involved.

This program was supported by JST.
