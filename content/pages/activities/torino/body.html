Visited to Polytechnic University of Turin

Multi-Scale Modeling Lab

In June 2026, I had the pleasure of visiting the Multi-Scale Modeling Laboratory at Polytechnic University of Turin, Italy. During the visit, I had valuable academic discussions with Prof. Eliodoro Chiavazzo and Prof. Matteo Fasano on multiscale modeling, micro/nanoscale heat transfer, and interfacial thermal transport. The visit provided an excellent opportunity to learn about the recent research activities of the SMaLL Lab and to exchange ideas on potential future collaborations. It was an inspiring and rewarding academic exchange, and I look forward to further communication and collaboration in the future.

Wentao Chen
Associate Professor

My research interests include extreme near-field heat transfer, thermal transport at the solid-solid and solid-liquid interface, and molecular dynamics simulation.
