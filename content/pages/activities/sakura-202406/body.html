Japan-Asia Youth Exchange Program in Science

SAKURA Science Exchange Program 2024

Kyutech held an international exchange program “Enhancement of International Exchange Partnerships and Global Engineering Education in Mechanical Engineering Field” under SAKURA Science Exchange Program sponsored by JST from June 9 to 14, 2024 at Tobata campus.

10 participants including 7 students and 3 faculty members from Qingdao University of Technology, Northeastern University (CN), Qilu University of Technology and Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati were invited to join this program.

International Joint Seminar on Mechanical Engineering 2024 took place to enhance academic exchange in mechanical engineering field. The participants enjoyed the 4 keynote lectures, 8 oral presentations. Events such as campus/laboratory tours and collaborative works have been conducted by 7 foreign students together with 22 Japanese students. This event prometed the international exchange and friendship of all the participants.

This program was supported by JST, Kitakyushu City and Kitakyushu Convention and Visitors Association.
