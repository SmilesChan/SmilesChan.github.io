China International Talent Exchange Foundation CEP Program Kick-off Meeting

Yangtze Delta Region Academy in Jiaxing, Beijing Institute of Technology

The China International Talent Exchange Foundation CEP Program was officially launched. The kick-off meeting for the “2026 Green Energy Materials and Devices Thermal Management International Internship Program” was held at Yangtze Delta Region Academy in Jiaxing, Beijing Institute of Technology, with participants including institute leaders, researchers, and internship students from Universiti Malaya (QS 56). The program aims to deepen international scientific cooperation, strengthen youth talent cultivation, and promote exchanges between BIT and Universiti Malaya in research collaboration, joint postgraduate training, and youth talent exchange, with a focus on green energy, advanced materials, and sustainable development, while supporting China-ASEAN cooperation and the Belt and Road Initiative.

Wentao Chen
Associate Professor

My research interests include extreme near-field heat transfer, thermal transport at the solid-solid and solid-liquid interface, and molecular dynamics simulation.
