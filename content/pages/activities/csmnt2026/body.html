Establishment Meeting of Micro-Nano System Thermal Management Branch, Chinese Society of Micro-Nano Technology

School of Integrated Circuits, Tsinghua University

On January 31, 2026, the establishment meeting of Micro-Nano System Thermal Management Branch of the Chinese Society of Micro-Nano Technology, along with the first session of its inaugural committee, was successfully convened at Tsinghua University in Beijing. Over fifty leading scholars and representatives in micro-nano thermal management from universities and research institutions across the nation gathered to witness this significant milestone. The establishment of this branch signifies Chinese creation of a dedicated academic community and collaborative innovation platform in the critical frontier field of micro-nano system thermal management.

Wentao Chen
Associate Professor

My research interests include extreme near-field heat transfer, thermal transport at the solid-solid and solid-liquid interface, and molecular dynamics simulation.
