Visited to Monash University Malaysia

Micro/Nanoscale Heat Transfer Lab

In September 2026, I visited the Micro/Nanoscale Heat Transfer Laboratory at Monash University Malaysia for academic exchange. During the visit, I delivered a seminar on “Molecular Dynamics Study on Nano/Microscale Thermal Energy Transport” and shared our recent work on thermal transport across nanoscale systems and interfaces. The discussion with faculty members and researchers covered molecular dynamics simulations, interfacial heat transfer, and related micro/nanoscale thermal management topics. This visit also provided a good opportunity to explore common research interests and possible directions for future collaboration between our teams.

Wentao Chen
Associate Professor

My research interests include extreme near-field heat transfer, thermal transport at the solid-solid and solid-liquid interface, and molecular dynamics simulation.
