2026 International Internship Program on Green Energy Materials and Thermal management of Devices

China International Talent Exchange Foundation, China Exchange Program for Young International (CEP) (Project Implementer)

To promote exchange and mutual learning among international young talents, the China International Talent Exchange Foundation established the China Exchange Program for Young International (CEP Program) in 2024. In accordance with the work plan, the application process for the 2026 CEP Program is now officially launched. The CEP Program follows an open and inclusive approach. It encourages mutual learning and shared growth, and selects applicants based on merit and practical outcomes. It supports outstanding young foreign talents to come to China for exchange visits, international internships, and short-term work, so as to help them better understand China and to promote mutual understanding and friendship.
